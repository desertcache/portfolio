Supersedes v2 (2026-07-26)
1. The 4-1-2 Grid is Final

The old fully-ramped grid ran six consecutive active nights with one deload. Wednesday BHA (attempted ~Aug 12, mid-purge) failed exactly where load math predicted — inside the longest active streak with no adjacent recovery. Rule: max 3 consecutive active nights; BHA always borders a recovery day. Do not re-attempt Wed BHA.

2. BHA Demoted to Luxury

Salicylic acid is a conditional recommendation in 2024 AAD guidelines with low-quality trial evidence; adapalene already owns comedone prevention with strong evidence. 1×/week is pore maintenance, not a pillar. 2× was never evidence-mandated.

3. Face Reapplication Codified

The q2h rule previously named body only. Face reapplication (balm in pack) is now an executable step with Diffey timing: reapply ~20–30 min into exposure, and after soak-through sweat. Brimmed hat added to hardware.

4. Photosensitivity Rationale Corrected

FDA controlled study found 2% salicylic acid does NOT increase UV-induced erythema, DNA damage, or sunburn cells (glycolic acid does). Adapalene is photochemically stable. SPF stands on desert UV alone — needs no help from actives.

5. October Gate Staggered

Requires a stable baseline first. Azelaic enters first, 3–4 clean weeks, then retinal. Two simultaneous new actives = unattributable flares (applying the one-variable rule to the roadmap).

6. Depth-Over-Breadth Path Named

Adapalene photoaging RCT evidence lives at 0.3%, not 0.1%. When more anti-aging is desired, the proven lever is concentration (adapalene 0.3% or Rx tretinoin) — not adding product classes.

7. BPO Trigger Clause Added

Benzoyl peroxide is the guideline's strongest-recommended acne active and was silently absent. Now a named omission with explicit trigger conditions.

8. Sunscreen Fleet Alignment

Updated to match live practice: Bioré added for going-out days; balm for field reapplication; CeraVe zinc confirmed on orbital-rim duty.

9. Vitamin C Operational Logic

Fridge storage, small bottles, discard at amber. AM wait compressible; missed mornings cost nothing (skin reservoir saturates in ~3 daily applications, ~4-day tissue half-life).

10. Occlusion Wording Precision

Softened from "dangerously" to "meaningfully" multiplies penetration. Direction was right, adverb overstated. Sunday-only slug rule remains unchanged.

Load Rules the Grid Encodes

Never more than 2 consecutive adapalene nights; max 3 consecutive active nights total (Thu–Sat); BHA borders a recovery day on at least one side; two full deloads per week. This grid was found empirically (Wed BHA failure) and is better-designed than the old end-state by the system's own load-management principle. Treat it as final.

Evidence-Based Frequency Note

4×/week is inside evidenced territory, not a diluted compromise — an RCT found alternate-day application of a retinoid matched once-daily adapalene head-to-head, and adapalene 0.1% monotherapy holds long-term maintenance against vehicle. Nightly would be the trialed standard but is optional, not owed. If skin stays quiet for months and you want more, the upgrade is concentration (see October gate), not frequency.

AM Routine — Defense Campaign

Identical every day regardless of evening schedule

Executable Sequence

  1. Cleanse: Equate Hydrating (Kroger Foaming on SPF-heavy / sweaty days).
  2. Vitamin C: Timeless 20%. Pat dry, give it 2–3 min after cleansing. Fridge stored; small bottles; discard at amber.
  3. Azelaic: SKIN1004 10% Ampoule (Phase 5; unpurchased — see October gate). Wait ~1 min.
  4. Torriden DIVE IN: Press in, let absorb (humectant layer).
  5. NMF + HA: The Ordinary. Thin layer; emollient cap. Let settle 2–3 min.
  6. SPF: ¼ tsp, no skimping. Fleet assignments on right.
  7. Eye Rim: CeraVe zinc to orbital rim every day. UV400 sunglasses + brimmed hat outdoors.

Rushed: Vit C → Torriden → NMF → SPF (skip azelaic + waits). Never skip SPF.

Missed Vit C: Costs nothing — skin reservoir saturates in ~3 applications (~4-day half-life). Don't double up.

Deep Dive Mechanics & Physiological Rationale

The biochemistry behind ordering, ingredients, and system constraints

AM — Defense in Depth

Equate Hydrating Cleanse. Establishes a clean canvas without stripping. Its entire job is to not be the problem.

Timeless Vit C (20% L-ascorbic + E + ferulic). The daytime antioxidant. UV that slips past sunscreen generates free radicals that oxidize collagen; L-AA is a sacrificial electron donor and a required cofactor for collagen crosslinking. Vitamin E covers the lipid phase; ferulic stabilizes the formula and doubles photoprotection (~4-fold → ~8-fold) — that claim is measured, not marketing. Needs pH <3.5 on bare skin; 20% is the top of the percutaneous absorption curve. Ops: fridge, small bottles, discard at amber. Reservoir logic: saturates in ~3 daily applications, ~4-day half-life — consistency wins, individual mornings don't matter.

SKIN1004 Azelaic 10% (Phase 5). The cleanup crew for PIH — selective tyrosinase inhibition in hyperactive melanocytes, anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial. Calibration: guideline recommendation is conditional and the trial base is at 15–20% Rx strength; at 10% cosmetic, expect help, not magic. Marks targeted are PIH, not true scarring.

Torriden DIVE IN. The humectant layer. Desert caveat that governs placement: a naked humectant in ~10% RH pulls water out — never the last layer. AM it's double-capped: NMF cream, then SPF.

The Ordinary NMF + HA. The emollient cap. Urea, amino acids, and PCA are the stratum corneum's own water-binders — restocks the reservoir rather than filming over it. Locks Torriden down; smooths the canvas for the SPF coat.

SPF — The Load-Bearing Step. UV drives the overwhelming majority of visible aging. The justification is the desert, full stop — it does not need the actives' help, and the old claim that it did was wrong on the science: in the FDA's controlled study, 2% salicylic acid produced no increase in UV-induced erythema, DNA damage, or sunburn-cell formation (glycolic acid did — and glycolic isn't in this system). Adapalene is photochemically stable; its label language is precautionary, and the practical concern is that irritated skin tolerates sun poorly — one more reason the load rules exist. ¼ tsp hits labeled SPF; the reapplication protocol above is what makes the label number survive an Arizona afternoon.

Eyes. Periorbital skin is the thinnest on the face and ages from UV plus squinting. Four levers now: CeraVe zinc to the orbital rim daily (mineral doesn't migrate and sting in sweat), UV400 sunglasses, the brimmed hat, and the nightly seal to the orbital bone. Retinal enters at the October gate — last in the sequence. Dedicated eye creams remain the seal's chemistry at ~6× the price — skip.

PM — Controlled Demolition + Rebuild

SKIN1004 Oil Cleanse (every night, step zero). Engineered for water-resistant zinc that gentle surfactant can't lift; still earns its slot on outdoor-day zinc, the daily rim zinc, and sebum. Net irritation load is negative — it ends the scrubbing residue otherwise demands. PM only.

Adapalene 0.1% (4 nights). The engine. Third-gen retinoid, direct RAR-β/γ binding, no conversion step. Normalizes keratinocyte maturation inside the follicle — prevents the microcomedone that precedes every pimple. Prevention at the source; maintenance RCTs show monotherapy holds the line long-term. The purge is this mechanism working, not failing. Anti-aging framing, honestly stated: the collagen/AP-1 story is established for the retinoid class and the photoaging RCT data for adapalene is at 0.3% — the 0.1% anti-aging benefit is a reasonable extrapolation plus SPF discipline, and the named upgrade path exists when wanted. Applied on fully dry skin only — hydration is a penetration multiplier, and on retinoid nights that buys irritation, not efficacy.

Naturium 2% BHA (Sat). Salicylic acid — oil-soluble, the only exfoliant that works inside the pore, dissolving the corneodesmosome glue on the dead cells lining the follicle. Adapalene prevents new clogs; BHA clears existing ones. One night a week covers that job. Never the same night as adapalene.

The Seal — Two Tiers, One Lane. The barrier is brick (corneocytes) and mortar (ceramides/cholesterol/fatty acids); actives strip mortar, the seal restocks it. The approach is directly validated: a double-blind RCT of ceramide skincare alongside an adapalene/BPO regimen showed lower TEWL, less dryness and erythema, and fewer inflammatory lesions than basic cleanser. And the trio matters — ceramides, cholesterol, and fatty acids are each independently required for barrier recovery, which is exactly Atobarrier's formula.
• Kroger Nighttime (volume tier): Post-ramp weeknight workhorse at ~$0.10/mL.
• Aestura Atobarrier 365 (premium tier): Nightly through stable post-purge baseline (~Sept) — tier delta is modest on intact skin, meaningful on a barrier running 4 retinoid nights in 10% RH.

Recovery Nights & System Rules

Recovery Nights — Deload. No actives. Wednesday and Sunday. Torriden on damp skin (max humectant grab), Atobarrier seal. Sunday adds the slug: thin Aquaphor, final step. Occlusion measurably multiplies percutaneous penetration — several-fold in controlled studies — so petrolatum over an active is a penetration amplifier pointed at the wrong target. On Sunday there's nothing underneath to amplify; it's pure water-loss blockade. Wednesday recovers un-occluded so mid-week variables stay clean.

6 System Principles
  1. Complementary Campaigns: AM protects collagen PM builds. Neither works alone.
  2. Streak Rule Enforced: Max 3 consecutive active nights, BHA bordered by recovery, 2 deloads/week.
  3. One Lane, One Resident: New purchases must name their lane; a staffed lane is a closed lane.
  4. Built-in Slack: Two deloads plus rescue protocol make the system antifragile.
  5. Depth Before Breadth: Upgrades deepen existing proven slots before adding new classes.
  6. Chemistry-Driven Ordering: Actives on dry skin; humectants never last in low RH; low-pH first for Vit C.
