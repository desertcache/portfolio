Fifteen delivery IPOs, from offer price to today

DoorDash, Uber, Grubhub, Deliveroo and eleven more, measured against the price their IPO buyers paid. Eight are above it, seven are below, and every one fell at least 38% along the way.

September 25, 2026Closing prices through Sep 25, 2026

Food delivery went public in waves: Grubhub and Just Eat in 2014; Takeaway.com, Delivery Hero, Meituan, Waitr and Uber between 2016 and 2019; Dada, DoorDash, Deliveroo, Zomato and Grab in the pandemic rush of 2020 and 2021; and Instacart, Swiggy and Talabat from 2023 to 2024. This page puts all fifteen on one scale: every daily close divided by the IPO price, from the first day of trading to the latest close, or to the last trade before a buyer took the company private.

Two names are missing on purpose. Uber Eats has no stock of its own; it trades inside Uber, which is included. Postmates never listed: it filed confidentially for an IPO in February 2019, and Uber bought it for $2.65 billion in stock instead, closing on December 1, 2020.

The short version

  • Eight of fifteen are above their IPO price, today or on their last day of trading. Seven are below, and Waitr went to effectively zero.
  • Every one fell at least 38% from a high. Instacart had the mildest drawdown. Seven lost more than 80% at some point, including DoorDash, which fell 82% between November 2021 and October 2022 and now sits 90% above its $102 IPO price.
  • The 2020 and 2021 peaks were the all-time highs in Europe and China. Just Eat Takeaway topped out at €109.65 in October 2020, Delivery Hero at €144.10 in January 2021 and Meituan at HK$451.40 in February 2021. DoorDash, Uber and Eternal (formerly Zomato) set new records in October 2025.
  • Most exits were sales far below the peak. DoorDash paid 180p a share for Deliveroo, which had listed at 390p. Prosus paid €20.30 for Just Eat Takeaway, less than a fifth of its high. JD.com took Dada private at $2.00 after a $16 IPO.
  • The consolidation is still running. Uber's €41.50-a-share offer for Delivery Hero is open until November 5, 2026.

Who bought whom

Most of these listings ended in a sale. These are the deals that ended them, plus the delivery companies that never listed.

  1. Aug 9, 2013Seamless and Grubhub merge. Both private; terms were not disclosed.
  2. Aug 4, 2014Square buys Caviar for a reported $90 million in stock.
  3. Oct 31, 2019DoorDash buys Caviar from Square for $410 million.
  4. Jan 31, 2020Takeaway.com's all-share offer for Just Eat goes unconditional (about £5.9 billion). Just Eat Takeaway.com trades from February 3.
  5. Dec 1, 2020Uber closes Postmates for $2.65 billion in stock.
  6. Mar 2, 2021Delivery Hero closes Woowa Brothers (Baemin, South Korea): $4 billion for 87%.
  7. Jun 15, 2021Just Eat Takeaway.com closes Grubhub, valued at $7.3 billion in stock when announced.
  8. May 31, 2022DoorDash closes Wolt. Announced at about €7 billion in stock; worth $2.84 billion at closing after DoorDash shares fell.
  9. Jan 7, 2025Wonder buys Grubhub from Just Eat Takeaway.com for $650 million, about 91% below the 2021 price.
  10. Mar 2025Uber drops its $950 million purchase of Foodpanda Taiwan from Delivery Hero after Taiwan's competition regulator blocked it.
  11. Jun 16, 2025JD.com takes Dada private at $2.00 per ADS.
  12. Oct 2, 2025DoorDash completes Deliveroo at 180p a share (£2.9 billion equity value).
  13. Nov 17, 2025Prosus completes Just Eat Takeaway.com at €20.30 a share (about €4.1 billion); the shares leave Euronext.
  14. Jul 16, 2026Uber offers €41.50 a share for Delivery Hero (about $14.8 billion for 100%). Pending; the acceptance period runs to November 5, 2026.

Data and method

Prices. Daily closing prices in each stock's own currency, as price change only (dividends excluded). Stock splits are adjusted. Waitr is shown per original share, adjusted for its 1-for-20 reverse split in November 2022, and its line ends on April 2, 2024, the day it filed for Chapter 7 liquidation.

Sources. Yahoo Finance for DoorDash, Uber, Instacart, Grab, Delivery Hero, Meituan, Eternal, Swiggy, Talabat and Waitr. Investing.com for Just Eat Takeaway, Deliveroo, Dada and Just Eat, with Dada's IPO day from Macrotrends' archived copy and Just Eat's first day and final weeks from a Yahoo-derived dataset. Grubhub comes from a public daily-price dataset. IPO dates, prices and deal terms come from company releases, SEC filings, exchange notices and news coverage. Every source is linked below.

Checks. Grubhub matches Macrotrends' archived series within 0.8% on all 1,552 shared days, and in its final year it traded at 94.5% to 101.4% of the value implied by the Just Eat Takeaway share-swap terms. Dada's two sources agree within 0.5% on all 1,160 shared days. Investing.com's Deliveroo series contains phantom prints at 25% to 40% of the real price; a continuity check removed 167 of them, and the remaining closes match a separate copy within 1.3% on every shared day.

Caveats. Just Eat Takeaway's series starts October 3, 2016, one trading day after its debut. Indian closes are Yahoo's last traded price, which can differ slightly from the exchange's official close. Returns are in local currency and ignore exchange-rate moves. Not investment advice.
