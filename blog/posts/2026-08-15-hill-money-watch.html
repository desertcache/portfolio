Hill Money Watch — August 15, 2026

August 15, 2026

A catch-up edition covering recent disclosures rather than a single trading day. Lisa McClain breaks a six-month silence with a private defense buy filed one day before a $3.2B state announcement; the Tier-1 roster is quiet; two members disclose large batches of late trades.

A note on this edition. This is a catch-up post, not a single-day digest. Nothing was filed with the House Clerk on August 13–15, so rather than report an empty tape, this covers the disclosures that have actually moved since the roster was last reviewed. Every item below is dated by when it was traded and, where confirmed, when it was disclosed. Regular since-last-business-day editions resume when the Clerk does.

The Headlines

Lisa McClain (R-MI) broke a six-month silence — and the timing is the story. McClain had not filed since February 6, 2026, after a period of roughly 1,200 trades a year concentrated in semiconductors and AI. On July 15 her spouse purchased $50,001–$100,000 of Saronic Technologies, a private autonomous-maritime defense company. The trade was disclosed August 5.

One day after that purchase, Texas announced Saronic would build a $3.248 billion shipyard in Brownsville, backed by an $80 million Texas Enterprise Fund grant. Saronic has raised $1.75 billion at a reported $9.25 billion valuation, holds a $392 million U.S. Navy production contract for autonomous surface vessels, and has been expanding its federal lobbying operation. The filing is listed as a spouse transaction, which under House rules means the member may have had no knowledge of it. NOTUS has separately reported this as McClain's second STOCK Act violation of the year.

For a name this dormant, the resumption is itself the signal — and it resumed into a private defense position rather than the semis-and-AI book she was known for.

Late Filings — The Compliance Tape

Policy Watch

H.R. 1908, the congressional stock-trading ban, passed the House 232–198 on July 22, 2026 and remains pending in the Senate. No Senate action surfaced this period. If it is enacted, the useful signal in this dataset shifts from buys to forced-sale notices — a very different tape to read, and worth being positioned for before it happens rather than after.
